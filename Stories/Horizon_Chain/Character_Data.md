# Northern Interior Extension expedition

The **Northern Interior Extension** is a Horizon Chain Authority field survey led by Maresk Toven. The expedition sets out from Gate Thaurin to survey and test a line of provisional nodes through Velu’s interior and establish a station at Point Golqik. Berrit’s departure ledger names nine people, including the expedition’s attached press observer.

### Julien Voss

Sex: Male
Age: 32
Nationality: S’Tsutodo
Occupation: Journalist
Affiliation: Dima Eta Register

#### Background

A thirty-two-year-old journalist with the *Dima Eta Register*, from the capital of S’Tsutodo. Known for writing about civic projects and road failures, he comes to Velu to document the extension. He joins as a temporary observer under a S’Tsutodo Civic Press credential. His field agreement requires him to follow Maresk’s orders, seek permission to access technical ledgers, and submit dispatches for review before transmitting them.

#### Personal traits and skills

Julien is observant and detail-oriented, with a keen eye for civic and infrastructural developments.

#### Notable Incidents



### Maresk Toven

A Veluan field commander responsible for the survey crew and its conduct. The crew calls him **Chief**. Deputy Registrar Pell explains that Toven’s command is an expedition appointment rather than a military rank. Maresk objects to bringing a journalist into the field and makes Julien’s place on the crew conditional on following the same orders as everyone else.


### Saffa

The expedition’s timing engineer. At the Horizon Office, he works on the tower-three timing correction. In the yard, he personally checks the travel lock and runs the oscillator briefly before departure. He explains to Julien how a station at Point Golqik would provide a fixed reference for positioning in the poorly covered interior.

### Qwen

The specialist responsible for field radios, batteries, receivers, and electrical power. He lets Julien listen to the Horizon Chain pulses at the office and is working under the second hauler’s open hood when Julien arrives in the yard.

### Orro

The mechanic responsible for the field haulers. Julien first sees him working on the lead hauler’s left brake. He calls Maresk **Chief** and is comfortable joking with him while the crew gets ready.

### Berrit

The expedition’s quartermaster and records keeper. He tracks supplies, load order, equipment inspections, and the crew manifest. He packs a spare mast sleeve against rain and records Saffa’s yard test of the oscillator over his objection.

### Hesk

The armed crew member responsible for what he calls **field discipline**. He helps Saffa load the oscillator and checks the rifle cases against Maresk’s list. He prefers his own title because he thinks the word “security” gives people too narrow an idea of where danger comes from.

### Nadiq

A guide who knows the country beyond the reliable surveys. Julien first notices him quieting a restless horse. He helps Pela fit the pack animals, and his exchange with Julien about maps shows how little trust he puts in what a survey leaves unsaid.

### Pela

The crew member responsible for the animals and camp loads. She fits and checks the pack gear alongside Nadiq and is blunt with anyone likely to mishandle the animals or their loads.
