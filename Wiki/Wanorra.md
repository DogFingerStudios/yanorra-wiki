# Wanorra

<span y-type="mapelement" y-zoom="3.2" y-center="-9.08, -51.76" y-minzoom="2" y-scrollwheelzoom="true" y-debug="true"/>

Wanorra, also known as West Yanorra, is the western continental region of Yanorra's Known World. It encompasses numerous nations, including [Ronobetu](./Ronobetu.md), [Duvessa](./Duvessa.md), [Bibi Shirif](./Bibi_Shirif.md) and the [Three Sisters](./Three_Sisters.md) and has a long history of exploration, commerce, and political development. Separated from [Eanorra](./Eanorra.md) by the [Brooding Sea](./Brooding_Sea.md).
