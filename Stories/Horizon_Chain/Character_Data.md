# Northern Interior Extension expedition

The **Northern Interior Extension** is a Horizon Chain Authority field survey led by Maresk Toven. The expedition sets out from Gate Thaurin to survey and test a line of provisional nodes through Velu’s interior and establish a station at Point Golqik. Berrit’s departure ledger names nine people, including the expedition’s attached press observer.

#### Role

Temporary observer and journalist attached to the Northern Interior Extension expedition.

#### Background

A thirty-two-year-old journalist with the *Dima Eta Register*, from the capital of S’Tsutodo. Known for writing about civic projects and road failures, he comes to Velu to document the extension. He joins as a temporary observer under a S’Tsutodo Civic Press credential. His field agreement requires him to follow Maresk’s orders, seek permission to access technical ledgers, and submit dispatches for review before transmitting them.

Julien was raised primarily by his mother who was widowed when Julien was only five years old. Julien's mother is a retired nusre who lives in Spencer, S'Tsutodo.

#### Appearance

Julien has a slim to average build, and is of moderate height. He has short, dark hair and wears practical field clothing suitable for travel and observation. He prefers flannel shirts and cargo pants where he keeps notebooks and writing tools.


#### Personality

Julien is observant and detail-oriented.

### Maresk Toven

- Sex: Male
- Age: 52
- Birthplace: Big Bay, Velu
- Native language: Veluan
- Other languages: Trade Speech (fluent)
- Occupation: Field Commander
- Affiliation: Independent

A Veluan field commander responsible for the survey crew and its conduct. The crew calls him **Chief**. Deputy Registrar Pell explains that Toven’s command is an expedition appointment rather than a military rank. Maresk objects to bringing a journalist into the field and makes Julien’s place on the crew conditional on following the same orders as everyone else.

#### Background

Maresk grew up in Big Bay, Velu, and joined the Veluan military at a young age, eventually rising through the ranks to become a commander. He left the military, and took a job with Horizon Office as a field expedition leader.

#### Appearance

Maresk is a tall and imposing figure with a strong build, reflecting his military background. He has short-cropped hair, a neatly trimmed beard, and typically wears practical field attire suitable for leading expeditions.

### Saffa

- Sex: Male
- Age: 27
- Birthplace: Big Bay, Velu
- Native language: Veluan
- Other languages: Trade Speech (fluent), Anqaran (basic)
- Occupation: Timing Engineer
- Affiliation: Independent
- First Appearance: Chapter 2
- Last Appearance: Chapter 11

#### Role

Saffa is the expedition’s timing engineer, responsible for calibrating the network's precise timing intervals and managing equipment like the oscillator and timing engine to ensure accurate positioning data.

#### Background

Saffa grew up in Big Bay, Velu, and showed an interest in mathematics and engineering from a young age. He excelled academically and pursued higher education at the Institute of Mathematics in Paz, Reddelstone with a full scholarship. After school he briefly relocated to Byad where he was recruited by Horizon Office. Never married and with no children, he technically maintains a home with his parents in Big Bay, though his work keeps him in Gate Thaurin or out in the field.

#### Appearance

Tall, delicate and somewhat lanky frame. Smooth baby face giving him the appearance of a tall teenager. He has dark hair, no glasses, and a permanent crease between his eyebrows that gives him a serious, scholarly expression.

#### Personality

Saffa is a meticulous, scholarly timing engineer with a dry wit and a deep, almost devout reverence for machinery and precision. While he can be sharp or exacting with those who misunderstand technical standards, he possesses a quiet, observational patience and relies on rigorous method to navigate uncertainty.

### Qwen

- Sex: Male
- Age: 44
- Birthplace: Big Bay, Velu
- Native language: Veluan
- Other languages: Trade Speech (fluent)
- Occupation: Tactical Communications Specialist
- Affiliation: Independent
- First Appearance: Chapter 1
- Last Appearance: Chapter 24

#### Role

Qwen is the specialist responsible for field radios, batteries, receivers, and electrical power. He lets Julien listen to the Horizon Chain pulses at the office and is working under the second hauler’s open hood when Julien arrives in the yard.

#### Background

Qwen served as a Veluan military radio technician until his discharge for unauthorized radio use when he tuned official receivers to monitor unmonitored atmospheric static for his personal research. His father, a Horizon Office administrator, used his position to get Qwen hired as a communications technician/specialist for field expeditions.

#### Appearance

Unusually tall and built like a folded ruler with a long, lean frame. He has nervous hands, a long neck, and a pair of wire spectacles that give him the distinct air of an underfed schoolteacher. His appearance is rounded out by ink-stained fingers from handling hardware, a tool roll slung across one shoulder, and a habit of licking his thumb before turning pages.

#### Personality

Qwen is a high-strung, fiercely protective junior signal man whose nervous energy masks a sharp competence with hardware. He approaches his work with a blend of professional pride and cynical pragmatism, frequently questioning authority while fiercely guarding the equipment he maintains.

### Nadiq

- Sex: Male
- Age: 44
- Birthplace: Baggenholm, Velu
- Native language: Veluan
- Other languages: Trade Speech (fluent)
- Occupation: Frontier Guide
- Affiliation: Independent
- First Appearance: Chapter 3
- Last Appearance: 

#### Role

A guide who knows the country beyond the reliable surveys. He first notices Julien quieting a restless horse. He helps Pela fit the pack animals, and his exchange with Julien about maps shows how little trust he puts in what a survey leaves unsaid.

#### Background

Nadiq grew up in the harsh northern frontier alongside his brother and father, a seasoned tracker who trained him in the "not-route"—reading subtle terrain shifts, animal behavior, and anomalies. A formative, psychological trauma in his youth involved a "Backwalker," a mimic entity that used family voices in the dark, resulting in a terrifying encounter that cost his brother his peace of mind. 

#### Appearance

He is a narrow, wiry man built from tendon and patience, possessing long dark hair tied back at the nape of his neck. His face features high cheekbones, severe features, and skin darkened by severe weather. Most striking are his pale gray eyes, which remain remarkably steady and watchful against his dark face.
