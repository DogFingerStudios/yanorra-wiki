## History

### The Mariven Refusal

For centuries, Hennamaria was a distant coastal province of [Bibi Shirif](./Bibi_Shirif.md), enjoying considerable autonomy due to its distance from the imperial capital. Its eastern ports prospered as important trading centers along the maritime route connecting [Barrel](./Barrel.md)via the [Barrel Islands](./Barrel_Islands.md) with the rest of [Wanorra](./Wanorra.md)

During a period of costly wars elsewhere in Dairgo, Bibi Shirif sought greater control over this profitable trade. Imperial authorities imposed new duties on goods arriving from the Barrel Islands and demanded that Hennamarian merchants surrender control of their harbors to imperial administrators.

The demands provoked widespread resistance. In what became known as the Mariven Refusal, local officials rejected the imperial orders, and representatives from several coastal towns established an assembly to govern their own affairs.

With its armies occupied elsewhere and Hennamaria geographically isolated from the imperial heartland, Bibi Shirif struggled to restore control. The rebellion gradually evolved into an independence movement, culminating in the establishment of Dairgo's first independent nation.

Bibi Shirif never willingly accepted the loss. Years later, it attempted to reclaim Hennamaria in the Winter Shore War, but failed to extinguish the young republic.
