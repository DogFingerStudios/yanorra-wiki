# Dairgo

**Dairgo** is a large island in the southeastern part of West Yanorra. The island is home to **Hennamaria**, **Nedma**, **Poempoon**, **Dudburia**, and part of **Bibi Shirif**.

## Geography and political divisions

The available wiki entries establish Dairgo's position within West Yanorra, but provide little detail about its terrain, climate, or coastline. Its political geography is divided among several countries rather than belonging to a single state.

The West Yanorra directory also lists the [Bharim Islands](./Bharim_Islands.md) and [Stanshonia](./Stanshonia.md) under Dairgo. It describes the Bharim Islands as an archipelago nation and a conduit between Hav and the rest of West Yanorra.

## Places of note

[Hennamaria](./Hennamaria.md) is located in Dairgo. Its entry names Thetbury as its capital and Levilbury as its largest city.

The [Bibi Shirif](./Bibi_Shirif.md) entry identifies **Qasr Marcel** as a significant religious and cultural center on Dairgo. It is home to the Great Mosque of Zayn al-Qamar and draws pilgrims. Bibi Shirif also claims several islands and land in the northwestern portion of Dudburia; the wiki describes only part of Bibi Shirif as being on Dairgo.

## Further information

The wiki currently gives no island-wide figures for Dairgo's area, population, or climate. It also has no dedicated entries for Nedma, Poempoon, or Dudburia, so their histories and characteristics are not detailed here.
