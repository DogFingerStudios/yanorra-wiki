# Eanorra

<span y-type="mapelement" y-zoom="3.2" y-center="-13.06, 33.17" y-minzoom="2" y-scrollwheelzoom="true" y-debug="true"/>

Eanorra, also known as East Yanorra, is the eastern continental region of Yanorra. It encompasses numerous nations, including [Velu](./Velu.md), [Barrel](./Barrel.md) and [Saple](./Saple.md).
